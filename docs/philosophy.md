# Philosophy

Every student practices, improves, and the teacher can prove it.
Means: calibrated competition, real stakes, honest measurement, real
rigor. A means is justified only if it serves the end.

## Constitution checks

1. Disengagement: the lower half keeps trying.
2. Boredom: the top half keeps caring.
3. Accuracy primacy: accuracy first, time as signal.
4. Fair shot: every student can reach a good outcome.
5. Honest measurement: scores measure what they claim.
6. Transparency: every score is explainable.
7. Mode: calibrated by default, harsh modes opt in.
8. No manipulation: no dark patterns, fake streaks, or forced urgency.
9. Teacher ownership: full export and delete control.
10. Calibration: the room stays engaged across the range.
11. Additivity: topics and questions are only added, never removed.
12. Naming: self-standing subject names only.
13. Trademark: no external program marks anywhere.
14. Copyright: original questions only, licensed stimuli only.
15. Training: no model training on protected content.
16. Firewall: generation stays inside blueprints.
17. Volume: every course holds its floor.
