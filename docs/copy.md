# Copy Deck

Every user-facing string uses plain language. No internal terms
(sequence numbers, ack codes, role names) ever render on screen.

## Speed-accuracy signal

- FLUENT: "You know this cold."
- GUESSING: "You are fast but guessing on this topic. Try slowing down."
- CAREFUL: "You know it, but fluency needs work."
- STRUGGLING: "This topic needs reteaching. Try practice mode."

## Tiers

Gold, Silver, Bronze, Rising, Practicing. Shared displays show rank,
delta, the top three names, a distribution, and your own trend. Lower
ranks stay private to each student.

## Session

- Reconnect: "Connection lost. Trying to rejoin without losing your answer."
- Draft saved: "Draft saved."
- Session ended: "This session ended. Your answers are kept."
- Resume: "Welcome back. Your last answer is restored."
